Goal

**Hypertables, continuous aggregates, columnar compression, retention.** The familiar time-series surface, on any Postgres 17+. No C of our own, no `shared_preload_libraries`, no restart. Apache-2.0.

What's in the spec

  1. Scale target

    **v0.1 is sized for 10¹⁰ rows in a single series table** on a single managed-PG instance.

  2. Architecture

    Each of the four big problems gets its own subsection here.

  3. Detailed design

  4. Feature mapping (for TimescaleDB users)

    A reference for readers familiar with TimescaleDB's surface, so you can tell at a glance what maps to what.

  5. Non-goals

    - Match TimescaleDB-class **8–15×** compression ratios on metric workloads.

  6. Acceptance criteria

    - **Install.** `\i pgseries.sql` installs cleanly on stock PG 17 and PG 18 in a single transaction.

  7. Open questions

    - Default chunk size: time-based (1 day), row-based (~3 × 10⁷), or adaptive?
